# Product Context

## Product

**AI Email Generator**

## Users

Students and professionals who need to write clear emails quickly.

## Goal

Help users create professional emails faster.

## User Problem

Users often know what they want to say, but spend too much time choosing tone, structure, and phrasing.

## Product Promise

Turn structured inputs (purpose, recipient, tone, key points) into a readable email draft that is ready to copy and lightly edit.
